LangBridge

phrase-to-phrase, page-to-page

A small built-in tri-lingual dictionary (~200 terms, EN-ES-FR) that translates words under your cursor, whole phrases in the popup, and the known words of a whole page. Everything runs locally in chrome.storage.local — no network calls, no accounts, no tracking.

Download ZIP

What it does

  • Click a word on a page (on http://127.0.0.1) to get its translation in a small tooltip, with a copy button.
  • Type a phrase in the popup and translate it word by word; unknown words stay and are reported honestly.
  • Click the floating LB button (or “Translate page” in the popup) to replace every known word of the page; click again to restore the original text.
  • From/to pair (EN/ES/FR) is remembered; EN ↔ ES and EN ↔ FR are direct, ES ↔ FR runs through the English pivot.
  • Interface in 6 languages (EN, ES, FR, PT, IT, DE) — picker persists.

Permissions — minimal and used

  • storage — UI language and the from/to pair live in chrome.storage.local.
  • Content script only activates on http://127.0.0.1 (a local dev server) — extend matches in manifest.json if you want it elsewhere.
  • No host permissions, no tabs permission, no background service worker, no network.
Honest note: this is a ~200-term toy dictionary, not a real translator. Out-of-dictionary words are left as they are and reported, never invented.

Install

  • Download langbridge.zip and unpack it somewhere permanent.
  • Open chrome://extensions and enable Developer mode.
  • Click “Load unpacked” and pick the folder (keep the folder after loading).
  • Open a local page, click a word, type a phrase, or hit the LB button.
